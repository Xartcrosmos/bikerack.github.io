Welcome to Bikerack

Secure, fingerprint-based access for public bike racks, with clear tools for administrators.

Scan the QR code posted at a bike rack to open a rack-specific page where riders can log in with their fingerprint or register in under a minute. This welcome page is dedicated to administrators who oversee racks, users, and activity logs.

You don't have an account? Claim your BikeRack unit now.

For riders at the rack

To unlock or manage a rack, stand at the rack and scan the QR sticker on the hardware. The QR code opens a rack-specific page where you can:

  • Log in using your fingerprint on the connected reader
  • Register a new account with your name and contact number
  • Use the same fingerprint at any supported rack in the network

Riders do not log in on this welcome page. Always start at the rack's QR code.

For administrators

Use your admin credentials to access a dedicated dashboard where you can manage racks, users, and activity logs.

  • Monitor rack status: available, occupied, or offline
  • Review and manage registered riders and their contact details
  • Track login and logout events at every rack in real time

SelectAdmin Loginabove to open a secure sign-in form. Only approved admins should proceed.

Reached here from a rack link?

If you tried to access a rack page directly without using a QR code, you may have been redirected here. Rack access is only available through QR links that include a device ID, so please return to the rack and scan the posted code.

Fingerprint and Log transparency

Every fingerprint login and logout at any rack is recorded with a timestamp and rack identifier. This helps protect parked bikes and allows administrators to respond quickly to incidents while keeping biometric data encrypted and access-controlled.
